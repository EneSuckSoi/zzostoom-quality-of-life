# Changelog

## 1.2.0 - sideways camera, game 2.7.2.0
- Camera: new "Sideways" setting that moves the camera to the right or left for an
  over-the-shoulder view. Aiming and lock-on are not affected.
- Checked with the game update (eldenring.exe 2.7.2.0): everything is found as before.

## 1.1.0 - status bars, movable HUD, pause fix
- Status build-up on enemies: an icon and a bar under the enemy bars while blood loss,
  frostbite, poison, scarlet rot, sleep, madness or death blight builds up. While a status is in
  effect (poisoned, rotting, frostbitten, asleep...) a lit icon shows it, with the time left.
  Shown only on the bars you have turned on.
- Buff list: position (X / Y) and "Grow upwards", so it can be moved away from Seamless Co-op's
  player names or other overlays.
- Focus bar position (X / Y).
- Buff timers now include Ash of War buffs (Seppuku, War Cry, Determination, Royal Knight's
  Resolve, Barricade Shield, Sacred Blade...): 169 named buffs instead of 107.
- Pause works: it now uses the game's own pause (thanks to techiew's PauseTheGame for the method).
  Map ghosts are no longer counted as other players.
- Muted, Elden Ring-like colours for the new HUD parts.

## 1.0.0 - first public release
- Camera: distance, field of view, height, lock-on range and lock-on camera tilt, applied live
  to every camera in the game.
- Combat HUD: Sekiro-style stance bars above enemies, focus bar, your own poise bar, enemy HP
  numbers and buff timers.
- Instant roll: roll on press instead of on release; keep holding to sprint.
- FPS unlock with a limit, higher refresh rate in exclusive fullscreen, FPS counter.
- Pause (only when alone in your world) and mute in background.
- Menu in 10 languages; buff names use the game's own translations.
- Controller menu shortcut: hold View (changeable).
