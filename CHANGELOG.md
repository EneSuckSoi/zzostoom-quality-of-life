# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - first public release
- Camera: distance, field of view, height, lock-on range and lock-on camera tilt, applied live
  to every camera in the game.
- Combat HUD: Sekiro-style stance bars above enemies, focus bar, your own poise bar, enemy HP
  numbers and buff timers.
- Instant roll: roll on press instead of on release; keep holding to sprint.
- FPS unlock with a limit, higher refresh rate in exclusive fullscreen, FPS counter.
- Pause (only when alone in your world) and mute in background.
- Menu in 10 languages; buff names use the game's own translations.
- Controller menu shortcut: hold View (changeable).
